Hi, I am **Se-Hyeon Hwang (황세현)**, an M.S. student in the Department of AI Convergence Network at [Ajou University](https://www.ajou.ac.kr/en/index.do), advised by [Prof. Jung-Won Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9sVITQIAAAAJ&hl=en) at the [Embedded & Software Lab](https://eslab.ajou.ac.kr/). I am preparing PhD applications, and my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/assets/pdf/cv-hwang.pdf) are available here.

My [research](/research/) is in **human-centered medical AI**: training and evaluating clinical models so that what they learn corresponds to clinical concepts and human values, and studying how clinicians and models decide better together than either does alone. That covers whether a model uses the concepts an expert would name, whether what it optimizes matches what the clinical task is for, and what actually happens when a clinician works with one. The medicine is not background to this: terms like clinically valid evidence have no content until the diseases, findings and diagnostic reasoning behind them are understood. My [study notes](/study/) cover both sides.
